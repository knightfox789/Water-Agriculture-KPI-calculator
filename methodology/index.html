Calculation framework

Methodology & Formula Register

A transparent register of the formulas implemented in the website. Field teams should use the scenario and measurement method that matches actual conditions and retain supporting evidence.

Common principle: programme vs control

For demand-side comparison, use the same season/year and comparable agro-climatic conditions wherever the governing methodology requires it. Normalize values to a common area before extrapolation.

1. Water Measurement

Pump

TH = SWL + FL + DH
Q (L/min) = 60 × HP × 75 × efficiency ÷ TH

Field depth

Average depth = (d1+d2+d3+d4+d5)/5
Water (L) = depth (mm) × area (m²)

90° V-notch

Q = 1.38 × H^2.5
V = Q × t

2. Water Saving

Same crop / crop change: Water Saved (m³/ha) = Control − Programme

Intercropping allocates programme water by crop-area share, compares water use per kg against crop-wise control data and multiplies by programme yield. Rainfed saving uses improved water productivity:

WP = Yield / Rainfall
Saving (mm) = R × (WPp − WPc) ÷ WPp
Saving (m³) = mm × area(ha) × 10

3. Additional Production

Same crop: (Yp − Yc) × Area

For crop change or intercropping, use Crop Equivalent Yield:

CEY = Programme Yield × Programme Price ÷ Control Price

4. Additional Income

Gross Income = Production × Price
Net Income = Gross Income − Relevant Production Cost
Additional Income = Programme Net − Control Net
This module follows project/Form 3C crop-economics logic. It is not represented here as a dedicated formula explicitly published in the reviewed HUF Demand-Side SOP.

5. Person-Days

Labour Days/ha = CACP Human Labour Cost ÷ Seasonal Daily Wage
Labour Days/qtl = Labour Days/ha ÷ CACP Yield
Person-Days = Additional Production(qtl) × Labour Days/qtl
The labour-days coefficient is a derived proxy from CACP cost/wage/yield inputs, not a directly published physical labour-days norm in the annex tables used.
Rainfed formula note: The implementation follows the governing methodology formula using WPp in the denominator. A worked example in the reviewed SOP appears internally inconsistent, so the formula itself is treated as authoritative pending formal clarification.
