Water Saving

Calculate demand-side water saving using the SOP scenario that matches the programme/control arrangement.

Same crop: compare programme and control water applied on a per-hectare basis.
Water Saved (m³/ha) = Control Water − Programme Water
Crop/variety change uses the same comparison formula. Enter old-crop water as Control and new-crop water as Programme in the shared fields below, then switch to Same Crop to use the numeric form. This framework keeps one source of truth for the formula.
Water Saved (m³/ha) = Old Crop Water − New Crop Water

Crop components

Programme yield is entered as the crop's production from a representative 1-ha intercropped plot. Control values are from comparable monocrop conditions.
Saving KL/kg = Control Water/Yield − Programme Allocated Water/Yield
